
SMARTAIR X1 - PRODUCT MANUAL

1. Product Overview
SmartAir X1 is a home air purifier designed to improve indoor air quality.
It includes a HEPA filter, three fan speeds, and an automatic mode.

2. Power Information
The device operates at 220 volts.
Its rated power consumption is 45 watts.

3. Operating Instructions
Connect the power cable and press the power button.
Press the speed button to select low, medium, or high fan speed.
Select automatic mode to adjust fan speed automatically.

4. Filter Maintenance
Check the filter every three months.
Replace the HEPA filter every six months.
Turn off and unplug the device before replacing the filter.

5. Troubleshooting
If the device does not start, check the power connection.
If airflow is weak, inspect the filter and replace it if necessary.
If the device makes unusual noise, check whether the filter is installed correctly.

6. Safety Instructions
Keep the device away from water.
Do not block the air inlet or outlet.
Unplug the device before cleaning.
